Post-hoc, only 4 training images were re-read with their original training annotations and original hashes verified. The labels regenerated by the converter exactly match the saved labels in byte-level consistency. The three repeated original categories and xywh coordinates are identical, with two groups differing in original occlusion attributes; this repetition arises from the projection of source annotations into the same category and same bounding box, and is insufficient to establish how a real-world target should be revised. In zero-height regions, the original behavior is `545,414,10,0,0,0,0,0`, with annotation row index starting from zero 88, score=0, category=0, width=10, height=0.

The original label parser rejects duplicate labels, and the original ignore component rejects regions with zero area. This round does not include deduplication, removal of zero-area regions, label modification, or lowering of inspection thresholds.

The conservative pilot policy declared a priori proposes isolating the entire existing refined_component for any sample incompatible with it; under this policy, 4 groups and 662 training images would be involved, leaving 3872 training images and 414 training_val images. **This is merely a proposed disposition that has not yet been applied, and the original 4534/414 split remains unchanged.** The 4530 individually passed images cannot be directly treated as a frozen new training set; formal contracts must still explicitly define the granularity and provenance records of sample and group disposition.

# # CUDA and subsequent steps
